# Summit Studios — website (v3)

Static site, no framework. The HTML pages are generated by one script; the output is plain files you can deploy as-is. Version 3 (October 2026) is modelled on Shopify Editions Winter '26: a fixed left sidebar with the edition wordmark and a chapter list, and chapters that each open on a full-screen Renaissance-style painting. Earlier versions are in `_archive/` and are not deployed.

## Editing

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Copy: projects, services, industries, reasons, FAQ | `_build/content.py` |
| Page layouts, chapters and their order | `_build/build.py` |
| The "Why Summit" fly-through | `_build/dive.py` |
| Styles | `assets/css/style.css` (tokens, type, layout), `assets/css/motion.css` (the fly-through) |
| Behaviour | `assets/js/main.js` (menu, clock, forms, filters), `assets/js/motion.js` (scroll and animation) |

After any edit, regenerate:

```bash
python3 _build/build.py
```

The script stamps every asset link with a content fingerprint (`?v=…`). `vercel.json` caches `/assets` for a year, so without a fresh fingerprint returning visitors would keep the old file. `.vercelignore` keeps the brief PDF, the moodboard, the notes, `_build/` and `_archive/` off the live site.

## Design system

- **Colours** (top of `style.css`): page greige `#DCDCD0`, panel off-white `#F7F7EE`, ink `#292919`, mid `#5C5C4E`, muted `#909083`. Pure white and black for the pill buttons.
- **Type**: Inter Tight 700 for the giant chapter words (120–220px, tracking −0.03em) and small bold labels; Instrument Serif for narrative text, with the first letter in Pinyon Script as a swash; Inter 500 at 14px for the header and labels. All from Google Fonts.
- **Paintings**: eleven Renaissance-style scenes generated for the site in `assets/img/paint/` (hero, websites, reception, whatsapp, software, industrial, clinics, trades, workshop, handshake, summit). They are original scenes, not reproductions. Device shots with the real client sites are in `assets/img/device/`, photos in `assets/img/photo/`, client screenshots in `assets/img/work/`.
- **Components**: `pill()` (white or black rounded button), `chip()` (small off-white link with an arrow box), `spot()` (bold label, serif narrative, chip), `feat_grid()` (bold label and bold muted description in two columns; label-only lists get three), `media()`, `sub_head()` (serif with swash initial).

## How a page works

- **Home hero**: the terrace painting with the headline "Ordinary Isn't Our Thing." The hero is pinned for three screens. As you scroll, a spark at the pointing hand opens into a ring of light. The painting turns into glowing line work and stars, and the Websites chapter draws itself out of the dark and rises in. This is `portal()` in `assets/js/motion.js`, a WebGL shader; the spark position is the `spark` argument of `hero_portal()` in `_build/build.py`. Without WebGL, or with reduced motion, the hero is a still painting.
- **Torn-paper handoff**: in the Websites chapter the page's paper rises over the painting with a ripped edge instead of the painting fading. Turn it on for any chapter with `paper=True` in `chapter()`; the edge is drawn by `torn_edge()` in `_build/build.py`.
- **Corner reveal**: in the AI Concierge chapter the next page grows out of the bottom-right corner over the painting while it is pinned (`corner=` in `chapter()`, `corners()` in `assets/js/motion.js`). It opens with an example call; the lines arrive as the page settles.
- **How we build it**: after the Websites chapter, the brush-spin clip (`assets/video/brush-spin.mp4`) stays pinned full screen while the seven process steps slide left along the bottom (`process_section()` and `PROCESS` in `_build/build.py`, `process()` in `assets/js/motion.js`). The clip pauses when off screen.
- **Growth Marketing (chapter II)**: SEO and AI search. A plain paper chapter with no painting (`chapter()` with `painting=None`); its torn top edge rises over the pinned "How we build it" clip. Content in `GROWTH_FEATURES`, `GROWTH_STEPS` and `ai_answer()` in `_build/build.py`.
- **Layouts vary by chapter**: `feat_grid(items, cls)` and `steps(items, cls)` take a variant: `fg--bento` (Growth), `chart_steps()` (Growth: a bar chart, pinned on desktop while the bars grow with the scroll, `chartSteps()` in motion.js; phones grow horizontal bars), `fg--panel`, `steps--list` and `fg--editorial` (AI Concierge), `chat_features()` bubbles (WhatsApp), `fg--spec` (Software), `fg--tags` (industries). The Websites chapter keeps the plain two-column grid.
- **Left edge**: from the second chapter on, chapter text starts 20px from the left (`--col`), since the index is tucked away there; the index arrow sits in the bottom-left corner.
- **Scroll feel**: Lenis at lerp 0.072 and wheel 0.88, a little heavier than the default.
- **Other heroes**: a painting fills the screen. The chapter list sits centred in a thin-bordered drafting box with faint drafting lines that draw on load. As you scroll it docks to the left sidebar.
- **Sidebar**: the "The Summit Edition" wordmark, the chapter list with roman numerals (the active chapter gets a dotted leader), copyright, contact and local time. Hidden on phones, where the list stays in the hero box. After the first two pages (the hero and the Websites chapter) the index tucks away and a round arrow on the left edge pops it out on a dark panel; a chapter click, a click outside or Escape closes it (desktop only; phones use the menu).
- **Chapters**: each has a sticky painting, a giant one-word headline and a serif lead over it, then the body. The painting fades out as the body's first sub-heading arrives, and the header and sidebar switch from light to ink text. Each chapter ends with a short rule and "Back to navigation".
- **Home chapters**: Websites, Reception, WhatsApp, Software, Industrial, Clinics, Trades, Work (the project index with three screenshots per row), Why us (the pinned fly-through), Start. Other pages use the same system with fewer chapters.
- **Scroll**: Lenis at its default feel (`lerp 0.1`), as on the reference.

## Deploy to Vercel

1. Push this folder to a Git repository, or drag it into the Vercel dashboard.
2. Framework preset: **Other**. No build command, output directory: `.` (root).
3. Attach `summitxstudio.com` and `www.summitxstudio.com`.
